# Module build contracts 03 — Services & Delivery; Operations & Support

Use volume 00's per-module intake, claim ledger, full explanations, diagram/capture, worked case, transfer task, and exact-ZIP QA. The current [Glean Delivery Methodology](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1hpOD3O2sHoM4loqY1WwQdtxjXHUHE0R5XQbdc9q9sMQ) is a starting point for phase/role research, **not** proof of partner scope for a given contract. Product support rights, SLAs, release cadence and UI must be reconfirmed with their current owners. R/T/V/P/X = research, ordered teaching, visual evidence, performance proof, and cross-update.

## Services & Delivery — ten modules

### SRV-101 — Partner Delivery Certification Process · Ready for LMS; local absent
- **R:** Retrieve actual certification course, current delivery eligibility/workshop prerequisites, services role authorization, shadowing/review requirements, competency guide, portal paths and renewal policy. Distinguish certification, course badge and client-delivery authorization.
- **T:** 1. Map who may lead versus observe a deployment. 2. Explain prerequisite skills, supervised work and evidence. 3. Walk intake, assignment, joint delivery, validation and renewal gates. 4. Identify when a partner must request Glean specialist help rather than represent itself as certified.
- **V:** Gate/role swimlane with proof artifacts and current portal submission captures; ownership boundaries are visible on every stage.
- **P/X:** Create a readiness plan for a two-person delivery team and reject an unsafe assignment; owner validates policy. FND-102 covers navigation, SRV-102 methodology, ACC-105 evaluates execution; rebuild if the alleged ready asset is an orientation-only slideshow.

### SRV-102 — Delivery Methodology · Ready for LMS; local absent
- **R:** Compare the actual ready asset to latest methodology, its phases, playbooks, delivery RACI by contract mode, customer roles and current readiness criteria. Confirm any adoption metric definitions and phase-specific owner changes.
- **T:** 1. Explain Plan, Deploy to Launch, Adopt and Measure as decision gates, not slide titles. 2. Detail outputs for realized outcomes, platform deployment/growth, agent building, training, governance and support. 3. Walk one customer from signed scope to steady state; show incomplete handoffs and how to recover. 4. Compare Glean-led, blended and partner-led responsibilities without promising universal ownership.
- **V:** Multi-lane delivery timeline with artifacts, acceptance owner, exception path and RACI; one fully worked project status packet.
- **P/X:** Construct a delivery plan and diagnose a missed readiness gate; assess clear ownership and risk response. SRV-103 deepens deployment, SRV-201 adoption, SRV-202 governance, SOL-204 change strategy.

### SRV-104 — Delivery Readiness & Kickoff · Planned; local absent

### SRV-105 — Workshop Facilitation for Use Case Discovery · Planned; local absent

### SRV-201 — Training & Enablement Playbook · Ready for LMS; local absent

### SRV-204 — Advanced Agent Debugging · Ready for LMS; local packaged draft
- **R:** Audit local nine-screen/five-question package versus tracker *Lab*, current Agent trace/debug docs, real available telemetry, safe redaction and support routing. Get Katya Alfaro or current technical SME to review failure modes.
- **T:** 1. Capture failing input, version, trigger, user, data scope and expected behavior. 2. Follow trace through retrieval/steps/tool/approval/output. 3. Distinguish source/permission, prompt, tool-auth, timeout and orchestration failures. 4. Repair or mitigate safely, run regression/negative tests. 5. Prepare a complete escalation package and customer update.
- **V:** Annotated genuine trace with timestamps and branch points; root-cause tree and side-by-side before/after run, not a four-box debug cartoon.
- **P/X:** Diagnose two unseen traces, produce redacted evidence bundle and verified repair; rubric requires correct root cause and no disclosure. BLD-207 creates tests; BLD-104 builds; OPS-104 general incident routing.

## Operations & Support — six modules

### OPS-101 — Admin & Platform Operations Runbooks · Planned; local absent
- **R:** Verify current admin UI, audit/usage/connector health surfaces, backup/restore and configuration options, roles, least-privilege access, daily/weekly checks and support boundary. Do not assume a health signal exists.
- **T:** 1. Establish inventory and ownership. 2. Run routine access, source sync, content quality and agent checks. 3. Log change and anomaly. 4. Triage or escalate symptoms with privacy-safe evidence. 5. Confirm closure and update runbook.
- **V:** Admin console captures for every taught click path, sample runbook with detection → diagnosis → action → verification; responsibility checklist.
- **P/X:** Complete a synthetic day-2 operating shift and fix an expired connector credential without overreaching. TEC-202 defines available telemetry, OPS-103 support handoff, OPS-105 change cadence.

### OPS-102 — Cloud Operational Runbooks · Planned; local absent
- **R:** Gather current AWS/GCP/customer-hosted operational guidance, network/credential/log ownership, escalation agreements and tested recovery procedures. Do not generalize one topology to all deployments.
- **T:** 1. Map cloud and Glean responsibility. 2. Recognize auth, DNS/proxy/egress, connector run and performance symptoms. 3. Collect correct safe logs. 4. Choose repair, rollback or Glean escalation. 5. Verify restored service and communicate impact.
- **V:** Failure trees over cloud topology and redacted log snippets at each branch; exact per-cloud screens only in cloud-specific appendix.
- **P/X:** Resolve two simulated incidents with timed status updates and evidence; score diagnosis and safe escalation. TEC-104 teaches network cause, TEC-106/108 reference deployment, OPS-104 coordinates major incidents.

### OPS-103 — Support Operating Model · In development; mapped external SCORM, no local source
- **R:** Retrieve external draft and current partner/Glean support agreements, Designated Support Contact rules, contact channels, service tiers, escalation, customer data handling and audit requirements. Check OPS-107/108 for overlap and unresolved partner entitlement questions.
- **T:** 1. Set support scope and who contacts whom. 2. Define intake, triage, severity/impact, ownership and evidence. 3. Separate how-to, defect, outage, feature request and unsafe access. 4. Track handoff and customer comms. 5. Feed recurring issues to CoE and enablement backlog.
- **V:** RACI/decision tree with evidence gate and three concrete ticket examples; real support portal capture only for actual procedure.
- **P/X:** Route six incoming cases, create a handoff packet and identify an invalid SLA promise. SRV-203 owns commercial service design; OPS-107 support portal how-to, OPS-104 incident command.

### OPS-104 — Incident Triage & Escalation · Planned; local absent
- **R:** Gather incident definitions, support playbook, severity/priority criteria, available telemetry and customer-communication rules; ask support SME for two anonymized production cases and a false alarm.
- **T:** 1. Stabilize and identify blast radius. 2. Reproduce using time, tenant, user, document/source and version. 3. Separate Glean issue from source identity, connector, network, agent/tool and data quality. 4. Pick severity/owner and mitigation. 5. Maintain timeline/updates, escalation and postmortem.
- **V:** Branching incident decision tree and annotated evidence timeline with privacy/redaction markers; genuine admin/trace captures where available.
- **P/X:** Run a table-top incident with changing evidence, produce customer update and escalation packet; human scores accurate severity and ownership. SRV-204 handles agent-specific trace analysis; OPS-107 actual ticket mechanics, TEC-202 signals.

### OPS-105 — Quarterly Release Readiness · Planned; local absent
- **R:** Verify release-note sources, actual release cadence and change controls, preview/GA status, security review, regression surfaces, enablement owners and tenant rollout variability. Do not present a quarterly feature schedule as guaranteed.
- **T:** 1. Triage announced changes against current sources/agents/admin settings. 2. Assess impact and permissions. 3. Re-test high-risk workflows in safe environment. 4. Coordinate change approval, training and customer communication. 5. Monitor rollout and roll back where supported.
- **V:** Change-impact dependency graph, versioned test matrix and annotated release-note-to-module claim map.
- **P/X:** Prepare release readiness pack for a fictional product change and find three impacted curriculum claims. TEC-105 provides promotion gates, BLD-207 regression strategy, SRV-201 communications, Change Monitor triggers content refresh.

### OPS-106 — Governance, Risk & Compliance Reviews · Planned; local absent
- **R:** Obtain current product security/retention/permission docs, customer-control evidence, approved legal/compliance statements, audit-log availability and actual governance cadence. Have security/legal review sector-specific claims.
- **T:** 1. Inventory data sources, users, agents/tools and owners. 2. Identify access, retention, external write and monitoring risks. 3. Test control design/evidence without claiming certification automatically. 4. Track exceptions, compensating controls and remediation. 5. Revalidate after product/source/policy change.
- **V:** Control-to-evidence matrix over system trust boundaries and example redacted review packet; distinguish customer-owned control from Glean-managed control.
- **P/X:** Conduct a mock quarterly risk review and reject insufficient evidence; hard fail for a secret or false compliance claim. TEC-102 security posture, TEC-103 identity, BLD-208 agent safety, SRV-202 CoE responsibilities.
