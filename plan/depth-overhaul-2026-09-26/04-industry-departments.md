# Module build contracts 04 — Industry Verticals; Department Solutions

All 16 tracker rows here are Planned with no local module source. Their source basis is a *research lead*, not a verified customer claim. For each play, require: two role-specific workflows, authentic but anonymized source objects, source identity/permission matrix, one no-fit or unsafe case, a discovery script, first deployment scope, measured outcome and a customer/industry SME review. Regulatory, privacy, clinical, financial, and public-sector assertions must be checked with current policy/legal owners; no automated advice or decision promise from a generic case. Use volume 00 for full source/claim/visual/capture/QA requirements. Avoid identical deck-derived playbooks with swapped industry nouns. R/T/V/P/X = research, teaching sequence, instructional visual, performance evidence, and cross-update.

## Industry Verticals — eight modules

### IND-101 — Financial Services Solution Plays · Planned
- **R:** Validate separate banking, wealth and insurance tasks with vertical SMEs, permitted data types, retention/recordkeeping, customer-access policy, approved references and current app/connectors. Identify a regulated decision requiring human judgment.
- **T:** 1. Contrast advisor service, operations knowledge and claims/underwriting support. 2. Walk source permissions across policy, client and internal research. 3. Build a human-reviewed question-to-evidence workflow. 4. Scope a narrow pilot and define accuracy, review and escalation metrics. 5. Explain why cross-client exposure or unverified advice blocks launch.
- **V:** Segmented user/data-access matrix, source-to-answer evidence chain with customer boundary, approved demo capture only from sanitized financial data.
- **P/X:** Design one bank and one wealth/insurance play; reject a cross-client leakage scenario. SOL-202 supplies pattern anatomy, TEC-103 permission mechanics, SOL-103 value model.

### IND-102 — Healthcare & Life Sciences Solution Plays · Planned
- **R:** Interview provider and life-sciences SMEs separately; verify source systems, patient/employee/research data classification, organization-specific HIPAA/privacy and quality requirements, approved references and permission model. No clinical recommendation claim without authorization.
- **T:** 1. Distinguish administrative knowledge work from clinical decisions. 2. Map persona/source/consent and minimum data needed. 3. Design policy, SOP and research/field-enablement workflows with verified evidence. 4. Add human review, escalation, audit and de-identification. 5. Measure process outcome without claiming patient benefit.
- **V:** Boundary diagram separating protected records, knowledge content and output; a worked SOP update with version/approval points, sanitized UI only.
- **P/X:** Scope two safe use cases and find a dangerous data crossing; clinician/compliance review required for any clinical-adjacent example. SOL-202 owns generic pattern; OPS-106 risk review.

### IND-103 — Manufacturing Solution Plays · Planned
- **R:** Map plant/field service, quality, engineering change and supply-chain workflows; check offline/mobile realities, EHS/quality approvals, OT/IT isolation, source versioning, role access and approved case evidence.
- **T:** 1. Follow a technician from equipment symptom to current manual and owner. 2. Show revision control, localized sites and permission-safe retrieval. 3. Compare service-ticket triage with quality deviation process; no autonomous safety instruction. 4. Scope pilot around document freshness and acceptance. 5. Identify failure when obsolete work instructions are retrieved.
- **V:** Revision and authority chain for a work instruction, plant/IT data boundary, annotated sanitized technician workflow.
- **P/X:** Build a plant-and-field use-case card with source validation and EHS hold condition; manufacturing SME scores feasibility. TEC-201 informs connector prerequisites, SOL-103 outcome baseline.

### IND-104 — Retail & CPG Solution Plays · Planned
- **R:** Verify store associate, merchandising/category, campaign and supply-chain tasks, seasonal data freshness, access by region/store, POS/catalog integrations and approved examples. Separate knowledge retrieval from transactional inventory claims.
- **T:** 1. Map store-floor question, merchandising policy and campaign launch to actual source objects. 2. Choose indexed versus live information for time-sensitive stock/pricing where supported. 3. Model store/region role permissions. 4. Add escalation for conflicting planograms or promotions. 5. Design rollout to stores and measure task quality and adoption.
- **V:** Store-region content/identity map and time-sensitive data flow, redacted example screens showing source timestamp.
- **P/X:** Design a two-store pilot and diagnose an expired promotion answer; reviewers score freshness and safe customer communication. SOL-204 supplies change program; DEP-107 marketing collaboration.

### IND-105 — Technology & SaaS Solution Plays · Planned
- **R:** Explore engineering incident, product decision, sales enablement and customer support workflows; validate repositories, ticket/docs and CRM permissions, code-security concerns, dev tools and approved customer proof.
- **T:** 1. Trace one incident across code, runbook, chat and ticket. 2. Contrast engineer, PM and support views and access. 3. Decide which tasks need retrieval, synthesis or agent action. 4. Define version/citation and human review for change suggestions. 5. Measure time-to-diagnosis/answer without promising code correctness.
- **V:** Cross-repository knowledge timeline with permission overlay and annotated safe developer/support demo.
- **P/X:** Scope one engineering and one GTM/support play for the same SaaS firm, with negative repository-access case. DEP-102/104 deepen each function; BLD-207 evaluation tests agent quality.

### IND-106 — Public Sector Solution Plays · Planned
- **R:** Research jurisdiction-specific procurement, residency, accessibility, records handling and approved security posture with public-sector/legal SMEs; identify supported deployment models and approved references. Avoid universal compliance and accreditation claims.
- **T:** 1. Separate citizen-facing, internal employee and caseworker workflows. 2. Map classified/restricted versus broadly shareable sources and identity. 3. Document procurement/security go/no-go and accessible experience. 4. Walk an internal policy-lookup case with human escalation. 5. Propose pilot evidence and stakeholder approvals.
- **V:** Agency trust-boundary/responsibility map and accessibility/records decision checklist; approved sanitized admin capture only if permitted.
- **P/X:** Write a secure pilot brief and reject an unsupported residency/records promise; policy SME review. TEC-102 owns technical proof, SRV-104 kickoff, OPS-106 ongoing control evidence.

### IND-107 — GSI Internal Transformation Plays · Planned
- **R:** Interview GSI practice, delivery, knowledge and partner leaders on internal reuse, client confidentiality, practice IP, staffing and Glean partner role boundaries. Verify cleared examples, no client names or proprietary templates without license.
- **T:** 1. Define internal knowledge, sales pursuit, staffing and delivery reuse cases separately. 2. Mark client-vs-GSI data boundary and engagement ownership. 3. Design a reusable offering with intake, templates and governance. 4. Link internal adoption to client service readiness while avoiding double-counted impact. 5. Explain how to scale across practices.
- **V:** Practice operating model with client boundary and reusable asset lifecycle; anonymized pursuit-to-delivery example.
- **P/X:** Design an internal-to-client accelerator and identify an IP/permission leakage; GSI practice reviewer. SAL-205 handles joint pursuit, SRV-202 governance, IND-201 accelerator production.

### IND-201 — Vertical Agent Accelerators Studio · Planned
- **R:** Obtain current AGT/Agent docs, selected vertical cases and official competency requirements; define reuse eligibility, licensing, configurable components, test fixture/ACL, measurement and human-review patterns. Do not claim official competency on LMS completion.
- **T:** 1. Select a validated IND/SOL-203 pattern. 2. Isolate universal workflow from sector-specific data, identity and policy. 3. Parameterize triggers, retrieval scope, tool contracts and approval. 4. Build an implementation package and two vertical variants. 5. Test allowed/denied, failure and drift; document onboarding and support.
- **V:** Component dependency map and two annotated vertical-specific execution traces, one showing a blocked unsafe action.
- **P/X:** Submit reusable accelerator, configuration guide, tests and value plan; two domain SMEs review variants. SOL-203 owns one use-case design, BLD-104 implementation skill, BLD-207 tests, BLD-208 governance.

## Department Solutions — eight modules

**Shared architecture, not identical courses:** each department course starts from a different authentic job, stakeholder tension, source inventory, failure state and artifact. SOL-101 owns the general persona/outcome model; each DEP course must teach what changes for its function. If a department has no verified current product use case or safe assets, mark `needs_sme` rather than fabricate screenshots or outcomes.

### DEP-101 — IT & Enterprise Apps Solutions · Planned
- **R:** Verify IT service, app-admin and identity workflows, ticket/CMDB/wiki access, provisioning boundaries, current approved integrations and incident data rules.
- **T:** 1. Follow employee access question to policy and ticket. 2. Distinguish knowledge answer from account change/action. 3. Map app owner, IAM and support handoffs. 4. Handle denied access, stale runbook and severity. 5. Scope a safe service-desk pilot with measurement.
- **V:** Request-to-fulfillment swimlane with approval/identity boundary and source permission example; sanitized ticket capture.
- **P/X:** Build triage workflow and reject unauthorized access-grant action. OPS-103/104 teach support operations; TEC-103 identity depth.

### DEP-102 — Engineering & Product Solutions · Planned
- **R:** Verify code/document/issue source behavior and permissions, developer-tool constraints, incident reviews, product-spec/version history and approved demos.
- **T:** 1. Trace question from codebase and spec to relevant owner. 2. Compare incident investigation, onboarding and product decision tasks. 3. Explain citation/version/staleness and private-repo boundary. 4. Decide search versus agent tool; human review for any code change. 5. Measure comprehension and diagnosis.
- **V:** Cross-source incident timeline with branch/permission labels, redacted search/citation or trace capture.
- **P/X:** Produce an incident-readout and incorrect-stale-spec diagnosis. IND-105 adds sector context; BLD-204 covers embedding developer workflows.

### DEP-103 — Sales & Revenue Solutions · Planned
- **R:** Review CRM/account, email/calendar and enablement permission models, privacy rules, approved QBR/account planning stories and current integration availability.
- **T:** 1. Gather a permissible account brief. 2. Separate source evidence, inference and missing data. 3. Build a meeting/prep and QBR workflow with ownership. 4. Explain external sharing approvals and CRM write boundaries. 5. Define quality and revenue-process metrics without crediting the tool for every deal outcome.
- **V:** Account evidence graph and safe outbound-brief approval path; redacted CRM-backed demo.
- **P/X:** Draft evidence-linked QBR outline and spot hallucinated/inaccessible facts. SAL-202 owns prospect discovery; SAL-205 joint account planning.

### DEP-104 — Support & Customer Success Solutions · Planned
- **R:** Verify case/knowledge/customer-account sources, entitlement boundaries, escalation and customer-facing response policies; obtain anonymized support examples.
- **T:** 1. Trace case intake to similar resolution and current policy. 2. Explain agent-assist versus automatic customer reply. 3. Handle conflicting or outdated knowledge, restricted accounts, and escalation. 4. Close feedback loop from cases into knowledge maintenance and CS playbook. 5. Measure time and resolution quality.
- **V:** Ticket-to-answer-to-knowledge-update loop with confidence/approval points and real redacted case capture.
- **P/X:** Resolve two synthetic cases, one unsafe for automation; score evidence and empathy. OPS-104 handles incidents, SRV-201 team training, IND-105 SaaS-specific case.

### DEP-105 — HR & People Solutions · Planned
- **R:** Verify onboarding, policy and employee-support use cases, HRIS permissions, sensitive employee data, regional policy variation, counsel-reviewed phrasing and approved examples.
- **T:** 1. Separate public company policy from confidential personnel records. 2. Map new-hire and manager personas. 3. Show a current, sourced onboarding answer and escalation on ambiguous benefits/policy. 4. Design support workflow with human approval for sensitive action. 5. Measure resolution and inclusion/accessibility.
- **V:** People-data access matrix and localized policy version chain; only synthetic HR data in captures.
- **P/X:** Design new-hire assistant and catch an access leak/policy mismatch. TEC-103 supplies identity, OPS-106 privacy review.

### DEP-106 — Legal & Compliance Solutions · Planned
- **R:** Validate legal matter access, privilege/confidentiality, document retention, policy versioning, approved review workflows, jurisdiction limits and legal owner sign-off. No autonomous legal advice claim.
- **T:** 1. Map matter team and document permissions. 2. Trace request to approved source and citation. 3. Mark review versus advice/decision boundaries. 4. Handle conflicting clauses, obsolete policy and external share. 5. Set audit trail and human counsel review.
- **V:** Matter-boundary and review/approval sequence with one denied-cross-matter example; redacted/synthetic contract excerpt.
- **P/X:** Create a counsel-reviewed information-request workflow and reject a cross-matter disclosure. TEC-102/103 own platform controls, OPS-106 compliance cycle.

### DEP-107 — Marketing & Content Solutions · Planned
- **R:** Review current brand/claims approval, campaign brief, product launch and asset-library workflows; validate reuse rights, embargoes, regional messaging and current integrations.
- **T:** 1. Find authoritative message and asset. 2. Distinguish approved copy from obsolete launch notes. 3. Draft campaign content with source citations and approval. 4. Coordinate product/field/region handoff. 5. Feed performance and content-quality findings back to owners.
- **V:** Content provenance/approval lifecycle with status and embargo controls; annotated approved asset search capture.
- **P/X:** Assemble a release campaign pack and detect an unapproved claim. IND-104 adds retail context; SAL-201 executive talk track; OPS-105 release-readiness trigger.

### DEP-108 — Finance & Procurement Solutions · Planned
- **R:** Verify AP/procurement policies, vendor/master-data access, approval limits, contract visibility and current connector/tool behavior. Find real but anonymized sourcing/expense or vendor-onboarding cases and finance-owner controls.
- **T:** 1. Trace purchase request through policy, vendor checks, approvers and system of record. 2. Separate lookup from transaction. 3. Explain threshold/authority and current policy version; avoid tax/accounting advice. 4. Handle duplicate vendor, missing approval or stale contract. 5. Measure cycle time and policy compliance.
- **V:** Approval swimlane with permission/amount gates and a redacted policy + request screenshot pair.
- **P/X:** Produce a compliant vendor-onboarding decision and block an unauthorized payment/write; finance reviewer. BLD-102 tool approval, TEC-103 access, SOL-103 quantified process baseline.
