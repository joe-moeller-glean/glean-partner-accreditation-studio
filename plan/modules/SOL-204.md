## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Dana handing off to Ravi
- **Through-line situation:** The pilot succeeded technically and nobody outside the pilot team uses it
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Ninety-two percent pilot satisfaction. Four percent org-wide adoption. The technology was never the problem.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Adoption curve bends: champion network, comms cadence, and governance rhythm each lift the curve visibly

- `OBJ-01` — Design a partner-led rollout with champions, comms, and feedback loops
- `OBJ-02` — Sequence adoption interventions against the first 90 days

- `OBJ-04` — Diagnose a stalled adoption curve and choose the highest-leverage intervention

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SEQ`** — Sequence adoption interventions across the first 90 days.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Champion network design, worked then independent.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Flat-curve diagnosis at week six - the signature moment shows the curve responding to the chosen lever.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — The four adoption levers.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Plan artifact, rubric-scored (tracker: `Plan artifact`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits a 90-day adoption plan: champion model, comms cadence, feedback loops, governance rhythm, and one diagnostic trigger with a planned response.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: treating training as the only lever, omitting feedback loops, no post-handoff governance.
