## Learning outcomes

- `OBJ-01` — Choose the correct Glean support path for a request: self-service, conversation, ticket, or feedback.
- `OBJ-02` — Build a complete ticket and assign the correct priority from business impact.
- `OBJ-03` — Escalate inside the Glean support portal so the incident stays tracked and SLA-eligible.
