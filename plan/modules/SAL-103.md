## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-CHECK`** — Unscored warm-up that calibrates the learner and seeds the post-exam review list.
- **`quiz engine`** — 20-item exam, one item per view, no timer, reshuffled on retry, explanatory feedback on the final attempt only, fully keyboard operable, result announced via role=status.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Exam, 20 items (tracker: `Exam`)
- **Blueprint:** Blueprinted across the Seller path: positioning x5, qualification x5, co-sell/registration x5, objections x5. Mixed recall/application/judgment; 200-level skews judgment. Balanced answer positions.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Drawn from real partner errors: registering late, leading with features, over-promising autonomy, mispricing, conflating referral and resell.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Post-exam personalized review list derived from missed items plus the learner's own confidence ratings.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Every keyed answer and every distractor rationale must be source-anchored and audited by Fact Integrity
- Items must not depend on the conflicted discount or sales-cycle figures until those rulings land
- Items test only what SAL-101/102/203 actually teach

Freshness window: 180 days; re-verify immediately if program economics change.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
