## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Exam + artifact (tracker: `Exam + artifact`)
- **Blueprint:** Exam: 24 items, 6 per domain (methodology, governance, adoption, support/run). Artifact: delivery plan with stages, gates, roles, RAID, adoption plan, and measurement. Rubric-scored. Both must pass.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Drawn from real delivery errors: gates without exit criteria, RAID as a formality, adoption as training only, no run-state ownership.
