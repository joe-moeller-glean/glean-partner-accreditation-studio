## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Sam and Ravi planning the rollout
- **Through-line situation:** Northwind wants to test in production because 'the sandbox is not realistic'
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Northwind proposes skipping pre-prod to save two weeks. Sam has to explain what that costs without sounding obstructive.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Promotion gates light in sequence; skipping one visibly propagates risk downstream

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Define a sandbox, pre-prod, and production environment strategy
- `OBJ-02` — Specify the validation gate each promotion must pass
- `OBJ-03` — Explain the risk of skipping an environment in concrete terms
- `OBJ-04` — Produce an environment and promotion checklist

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Validation checks assigned to the correct promotion gate.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Skip-pre-prod decision; the signature reveal propagates the resulting risk visibly downstream.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Environment purposes.
- **`INT-CHECKLIST`** — The learner builds the promotion checklist that is then scored.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Checklist review, rubric-scored (tracker: `Checklist review`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits an environment and promotion checklist; rubric checks required elements per gate, risk articulation, and fit to a stated constraint.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: treating sandbox as pre-prod, promoting without permission validation, no rollback plan.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Environment and promotion checklist with per-gate validation criteria.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Available environment options and what genuinely differs between them
- Validation criteria that actually matter before production promotion
- Rollback capabilities, if any - do not imply a rollback that does not exist

Freshness window: 90 days (product configuration).. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
