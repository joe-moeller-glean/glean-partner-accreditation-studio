## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Priya, newly appointed partner practice lead at a mid-size VAR
- **Through-line situation:** Priya must decide whether to build a Glean practice, and what her firm gets for it
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Priya's leadership asks: 'Why should we invest a quarter of practice capacity in Glean?' She has 48 hours to answer with numbers, not enthusiasm.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Program-value card assembles from four pieces (resell, services, support, market position) as the learner completes the sort

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Describe the Glean partner program's routes to market and state which one fits a given partner firm
- `OBJ-02` — Distinguish reseller, services, and referral motions by what the partner delivers and how they are compensated
- `OBJ-03` — Identify the enablement, GTM, co-marketing, and expertise support available to a partner
- `OBJ-04` — Locate the program resources a new partner needs in their first 30 days

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Six partner-firm profile cards sorted into resell / services / referral. Keyboard: select card, arrow to bucket, Enter. Live region announces each placement. Feedback explains why the firm fits.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Four support pillars on the program diagram; each opens a panel naming the concrete benefit. 44px targets, aria-expanded.
- **`INT-CHECK`** — One retrieval item plus a two-point confidence rating that feeds the review list on the job-aid screen.
- **`INT-CHECKLIST`** — First-30-days checklist the learner ticks; state persists and the summary downloads locally.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Quiz, 8 items (tracker: `Quiz`)
- **Blueprint:** OBJ-01 x2 recall, OBJ-02 x3 application (firm profile -> motion), OBJ-03 x2 recall, OBJ-04 x1 application. Positions balanced.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Real partner confusions surfaced in research: conflating referral with resell, assuming services partners get resale margin, assuming enablement is self-serve only.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- One-page 'New Glean partner: first 30 days' - route-to-market decision rule, named support levers, where to find deal registration and enablement, who to contact.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Program launch timing and current routes to market (resell / services / referral) - exact scope of each
- Discount and margin structure - CONFLICTED in sources (10%/25% vs 25%+ plus comp neutrality); needs program-owner ruling before any figure ships
- Named support categories (enablement, GTM, co-marketing, insights) and the concrete items under each
- Any partner-economics metric (deal size, NRR, renewal, services drag) requires a tier-1/tier-2 anchor or is cut per PR-01

Freshness window: 180 days (program/policy). Discount and metric claims are the blocking items.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
