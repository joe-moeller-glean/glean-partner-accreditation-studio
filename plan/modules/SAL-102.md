## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Marcus, with Dana as the technical second chair
- **Through-line situation:** Marcus must demo to a mixed Northwind audience with eight minutes before the CIO leaves
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Eight minutes. A CIO who will leave early, an engineer who will interrupt, and a demo environment Marcus has not opened in a week.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Demo arc timeline compresses from 30 minutes to 8 while keeping the required beats

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Deliver the demo arc in the correct order with a stated purpose per beat
- `OBJ-02` — Adapt the arc for a shortened slot without dropping a required beat
- `OBJ-03` — Handle the three most common demo interruptions without losing the thread
- `OBJ-04` — Prepare a demo environment and verify it before a customer call

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SIM`** — Two guided walkthroughs over real captures, one beat per step, <=35 words each.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Two live interruption scenarios (CIO leaving, engineer challenging) with recovery consequences.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Compress the arc to 8 minutes by selecting which beats survive; feedback explains what breaks when a required beat is cut.
- **`INT-CHECKLIST`** — Pre-demo verification checklist, downloadable.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Demo rubric (tracker: `Demo rubric`) (tracker: `Demo rubric`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner records or self-attests a demo run; rubric scores arc order, purpose articulation, interruption handling, time discipline, and factual accuracy. Module records submission; human scores.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: showing features without a purpose statement, answering a permissions challenge with a feature list, dropping the outcome beat to save time.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Demo arc card with per-beat purpose, the 8-minute compression rule, three interruption responses, and the pre-demo checklist.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Current demo surfaces, navigation, and capabilities shown in each beat
- Any proof point or statistic used in a talk track needs a tier-1/tier-2 anchor
- Permission behavior, since the interruption scenario depends on answering it correctly

Freshness window: 90 days (product behavior + captures).. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
