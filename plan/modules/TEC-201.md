## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Sam preparing a connector rollout
- **Through-line situation:** Six connectors requested, and four are missing an app owner or the right scopes
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Six connector requests. Four will fail at first crawl for reasons that were knowable in week one.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Connector readiness board resolves: each request turns green only when every prerequisite is satisfied

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Specify the prerequisites for a connector before implementation begins
- `OBJ-02` — Identify the app owner, auth method, and scopes required for a given source
- `OBJ-03` — Define the validation criteria that prove a connector is working correctly
- `OBJ-04` — Produce a connector deployment checklist per source

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Six sources matched to their prerequisite sets.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Checklist construction, worked then independent.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Reduced-scopes decision; branches show the crawl and permission consequences.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Connector lifecycle stages.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Checklist review, rubric-scored (tracker: `Checklist review`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits a per-source connector checklist: app owner, auth method, scopes, validation criteria, and the go/no-go rule.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: starting without an app owner, assuming default scopes suffice, declaring success on a crawl completion rather than validated content.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Connector deployment checklist, one column per source.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Real prerequisites, auth methods, and scope requirements per connector family - tier-1 docs only
- Crawl and validation behavior that proves correct operation
- Rate limits or quotas, if stated, need a tier-1 anchor

Freshness window: 90 days (product configuration).. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.

## 9. Risks, blockers, and SME questions

- Required for the Solution Architect badge; also unblocks BLD-201.
- `Content Requests` source with no asset - net-new from product documentation.
- Connector specifics vary per source; scope the module to auth/scope/validation patterns rather than enumerating every connector, or it will be permanently stale.
- No owner - `needs_sme`.
