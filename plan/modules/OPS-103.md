## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Nia and the partner's support lead
- **Through-line situation:** Support requests arrive through four channels with no triage model
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Four intake channels, no severity model, and the same question answered three different ways last week.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Support model resolves: tiers, intake, and escalation lock into one operating model

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Define L1 and L2 support responsibilities and boundaries
- `OBJ-02` — Establish intake, triage, and severity criteria
- `OBJ-03` — Collect the evidence required for each escalation level
- `OBJ-04` — Escalate to Glean correctly and at the right time
