## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-MATRIX`** — Prioritized portfolio submission with scored dimensions.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Value model and blueprint submissions built from the case brief.
- **`INT-CHECKLIST`** — Adoption plan submission.
- **`INT-CHECK`** — Readiness self-check.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Case study, rubric-scored (tracker: `Case study`)
- **Blueprint:** Four deliverables scored against one rubric: completeness, factual accuracy, fit to the case constraints, risk handling, and communicability. Pass = 80% with no criterion insufficient. Human-scored.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Not applicable - artifact assessment. The readiness self-check carries corrective feedback.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Case submission checklist plus the full scoring rubric, published in advance.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- The case brief must use a fictional but realistic customer; no real customer data
- Any figure the case supplies must be internally consistent and clearly marked as case-provided
- Rubric criteria must be objective enough for consistent human scoring

Freshness window: 365 days for the case; 90 days for any product capability the case assumes.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
