## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Better-together plays sorted by whether they require the cloud partner.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Two-stage joint-plan construction with owner assignment; the signature reveal renders the learner's plan.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Ownership conflict resolution with consequences for deal velocity.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Joint swimlane motions.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Plan artifact, rubric-scored (tracker: `Plan artifact`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits a joint account plan: ICP alignment, per-motion owners, better-together plays, shared metrics, cadence, and one named risk with a mitigation.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: leaving motions unowned, choosing metrics nobody can measure, escalating an ownership conflict before attempting alignment.
