## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Dana, solution advisor, facilitating her first discovery workshop
- **Through-line situation:** Northwind names one use case; Dana must leave the room with a scored, sequenced portfolio
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Northwind says 'we want AI for support tickets.' Ninety minutes later Dana has eleven use cases across four departments. Which three go first?
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** The scatter of eleven candidate use cases resolves into a ranked, sequenced roadmap

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Run a structured discovery conversation that surfaces use cases across more than one team
- `OBJ-02` — Score a candidate use case on value, feasibility, and data readiness
- `OBJ-03` — Sequence a use-case portfolio so early wins unlock later ones
- `OBJ-04` — Produce a prioritized use-case worksheet a customer will recognize as their own

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-MATRIX`** — Score three use cases on two axes using numeric selects (no drag required); scores persist and drive the sequencing screen.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Two-stage scorecard fade-out before the learner scores independently.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Quick-win vs foundation-first sequencing decision with real downstream consequences.
- **`INT-SIM`** — Discovery question ladder walked one question at a time.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Workshop artifact, rubric-scored (tracker: `Workshop artifact`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits a prioritized worksheet: >=6 use cases, >=2 departments, scores on all dimensions, a sequence with stated dependencies, and one explicit deprioritization with reasoning.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: scoring only on value, ignoring data readiness, sequencing by enthusiasm rather than dependency.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Prioritized use-case worksheet plus the discovery question sequence and the scoring rubric.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Departmental use-case inventory from the Departmental Outcomes master deck - which are current and demonstrable
- Any stated outcome or benefit per use case needs a tier-1/tier-2 anchor (PR-01) or is framed qualitatively
- Data-readiness prerequisites that genuinely gate a use case

Freshness window: 365 days conceptual; 90 days for any product-capability claim inside a use case.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
