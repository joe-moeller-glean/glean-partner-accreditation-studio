## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Sam, before implementation starts
- **Through-line situation:** Northwind's identity setup will determine whether permissions work correctly on day one
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Northwind has three identity providers, two of them partially migrated. Get this wrong and every permission check inherits the error.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Identity map resolves: users, groups, and roles align across systems into one governed boundary

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Specify the SSO and identity prerequisites for an implementation
- `OBJ-02` — Explain how people data and group membership affect permissions and personalization
- `OBJ-03` — Define role boundaries and administrative separation of duties
- `OBJ-04` — Produce an identity and governance prerequisite checklist

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Admin actions sorted by role to establish separation of duties.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Proceed-or-block decision on a partial identity migration; branches show the day-one permission consequences.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Identity prerequisites and permission derivation.
- **`INT-CHECKLIST`** — Prerequisite checklist, downloadable.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.
