## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Ravi facilitating rather than presenting
- **Through-line situation:** A discovery workshop where the customer must do the thinking
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Ravi presented for fifty minutes. The customer agreed with everything and committed to nothing.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Facilitation arc resolves: talk time drops as customer output rises

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Design a workshop agenda with explicit outputs per segment
- `OBJ-02` — Facilitate so the customer produces the artifact, not the consultant
- `OBJ-03` — Handle a dominant participant and a silent room
- `OBJ-04` — Close a workshop with agreed decisions and owners

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-COMPARE`** — Presenting versus facilitating; the signature inversion shows talk time dropping as output rises.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Dominant-participant and silent-room interventions.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Agenda design with outputs per segment.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Closing techniques sorted by whether they produce commitment.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Observed facilitation, rubric-scored (tracker: `Observed facilitation`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner facilitates a session (live or recorded) and submits the agenda plus customer-produced outputs. Rubric: output per segment, talk-time discipline, intervention handling, decisions with owners.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: segments without outputs, presenting through the workshop, closing without owners.
