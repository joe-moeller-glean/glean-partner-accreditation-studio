## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Priya, now quoting her first Glean deal
- **Through-line situation:** Priya must structure a commercial proposal with services attached and does not know her approval boundaries
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Priya's proposal has a number in it she is not authorized to offer. She finds out at the approval gate.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Deal structure assembles: license, services, and margin components snap into an approved shape

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Explain the commercial packaging model and what drives price
- `OBJ-02` — Structure a partner proposal with an appropriate services attach
- `OBJ-03` — Identify which commercial terms require Glean approval before being offered
- `OBJ-04` — Calculate partner economics for a given deal shape

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-FADE`** — Three-stage economics model; the learner computes partner margin for a given deal shape.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Terms sorted into 'partner may offer' vs 'requires Glean approval'.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Late-stage discount pressure with consequences for margin and approval integrity.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Deal anatomy components.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Knowledge check, 6 items (tracker: `Knowledge check`)
- **Blueprint:** OBJ-01 x2, OBJ-02 x1, OBJ-03 x2 (approval boundary), OBJ-04 x1 (calculation).
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Assuming any discount is partner-discretionary, forgetting services attach, conflating referral fee with resale margin.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Commercial structuring guide - price drivers, deal anatomy, approval boundaries, and a margin worksheet.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Current packaging model and price drivers - 90-day window, pricing class
- Discount and margin structure - CONFLICTED across sources; blocking. Needs program-owner ruling
- Which terms require Glean approval - must come from a designated system of record, not a deck

Freshness window: 90 days (pricing and packaging).. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.

## 9. Risks, blockers, and SME questions

- Pricing content with no asset and no owner - the highest-stakes `needs_sme` in the Seller path.
- Do not ship any number without a system-of-record anchor; qualitative framing is acceptable, invented figures are not.
- Approval boundaries are policy; a wrong statement here has commercial consequences. Escalate rather than infer.
