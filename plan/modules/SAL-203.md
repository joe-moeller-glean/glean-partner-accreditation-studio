## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Marcus, in a competitive deal
- **Through-line situation:** Northwind is comparing Glean against a bundled AI tool their CIO already owns
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** 'We already pay for an AI assistant in our productivity suite. Convince me not to just turn it on.'
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Objection response builder assembles the learner's chosen frame, evidence, and redirect into one talk track

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Differentiate Glean on verifiable capability rather than disparagement
- `OBJ-02` — Respond to the five most common objections with a frame, evidence, and redirect
- `OBJ-03` — Choose whether to lead with search, assistant, or agent outcomes for a given competitor context
- `OBJ-04` — Recognize when to escalate a competitive claim instead of answering it

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Four objection branches; each shows the credibility cost of disparagement versus a verifiable frame.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Five competitor contexts sorted by which outcome to lead with.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Response builder - the signature moment renders the learner's own frame/evidence/redirect.
- **`INT-REVEAL`** — Myth-versus-reality pairs on common competitor claims.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Scenario quiz, 10 items (tracker: `Scenario quiz`)
- **Blueprint:** OBJ-01 x2, OBJ-02 x4 (objection scenarios), OBJ-03 x3 (lead-with judgment), OBJ-04 x1 (escalation). All stems are situations, not definitions.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Disparaging the competitor, citing an unverifiable capability gap, answering a legal/security question outside the seller's authority, leading with agents to a search-oriented buyer.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Objection response card - five objections with frame, verifiable evidence, redirect, and the escalation trigger.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Every competitive statement must be verifiable and current; connector-count comparisons inherit the PR-02 single-claim rule
- Security and data-handling responses must match current posture (90-day window)
- No competitor marks, logos, or unverified capability assertions in any asset

Freshness window: 90 days - competitive and security claims decay fastest.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
