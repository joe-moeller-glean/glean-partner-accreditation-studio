## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Ravi, delivery lead at a services partner
- **Through-line situation:** Ravi must get his firm certified to deliver Glean implementations
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Ravi's firm has three Glean deals in pipeline and no certified delivery capability. The certification path is the gate.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Certification path assembles: prerequisites, evidence, and review stages in sequence

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Describe the partner delivery certification stages and their requirements
- `OBJ-02` — Identify the evidence a firm must produce at each stage
- `OBJ-03` — Plan a realistic path to certification for a given firm
- `OBJ-04` — Explain how certification relates to delivery engagement eligibility
