## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Nia, platform admin at the customer, supported by the partner
- **Through-line situation:** Week two after go-live and routine operations are undefined
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Nia inherited the platform on Friday. By Monday she has eleven requests and no runbook.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Operations calendar assembles: daily, weekly, and monthly duties settle into a rhythm

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Perform routine day-2 administrative workflows correctly
- `OBJ-02` — Run the recurring platform health checks
- `OBJ-03` — Recognize when a routine task requires escalation
- `OBJ-04` — Maintain an operations runbook that a new admin can follow

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SIM`** — Two guided walkthroughs: daily health check and a routine admin task.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Requests classified as routine versus requiring escalation.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Operations calendar duties - the signature rhythm.
- **`INT-CHECKLIST`** — Operations runbook the learner builds.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Quiz, 8 items (tracker: `Quiz`)
- **Blueprint:** OBJ-01 x3, OBJ-02 x3, OBJ-03 x1, OBJ-04 x1.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Escalating routine tasks, skipping health checks, assuming no news means healthy.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Operations runbook with the daily/weekly/monthly duty calendar.
