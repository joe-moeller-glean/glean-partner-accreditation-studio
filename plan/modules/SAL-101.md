## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Marcus, partner account executive
- **Through-line situation:** Marcus has a Northwind relationship and a Glean AE he has never met
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Marcus finds a Glean opportunity in his own account - and immediately does the two things that make the Glean AE stop trusting him.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Co-sell swimlane assembles: partner motions and Glean motions interlock across one deal timeline

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Position Glean to a business buyer in terms of outcomes rather than features
- `OBJ-02` — Identify the buying signals that indicate a real Glean opportunity
- `OBJ-03` — Run the co-sell motion: registration, account mapping, and joint pursuit hygiene
- `OBJ-04` — Align with the Glean account team on swimlanes and shared metrics
