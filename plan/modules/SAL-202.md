## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Marcus and Dana running discovery together
- **Through-line situation:** Northwind's stakeholders each describe a different problem and none of them is the real one
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Four stakeholders, four different 'main problem' answers. Marcus has one hour to find the one that pays for the project.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Pain map resolves: scattered symptoms converge onto two root workflow bottlenecks

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Run a structured discovery sequence that separates symptom from root cause
- `OBJ-02` — Map stakeholder pain to a specific workflow bottleneck
- `OBJ-03` — Convert discovered pain into a testable use-case hypothesis
- `OBJ-04` — Produce a discovery worksheet that survives handoff to a solution advisor

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SIM`** — Two stakeholder interviews walked question by question; the learner selects the next question and sees what it surfaces.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Symptoms mapped onto workflow bottlenecks - the signature payoff.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Hypothesis statement completed from a partial template.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Scope-expansion vs pilot-protection decision.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Worksheet submission, rubric-scored (tracker: `Worksheet submission`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits a completed discovery worksheet: stakeholders, symptoms, mapped bottlenecks, one testable hypothesis, and the disqualifying evidence they would look for.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: accepting the first stated problem, mapping pain to a product feature rather than a workflow, writing an untestable hypothesis.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Discovery worksheet plus the question ladder and the symptom-vs-root-cause test.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Discovery framing must not assert product capability that does not exist; every capability referenced needs an anchor
- Workflow bottleneck categories should align with the Departmental Outcomes source material

Freshness window: 365 days conceptual; 90 days for capability references.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
