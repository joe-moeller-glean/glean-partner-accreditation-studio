## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Exam + lab (tracker: `Exam + lab`)
- **Blueprint:** Exam: 20 items across admin ops, support model, and governance. Lab: multi-cause incident with a required escalation package and customer comms. Rubric-scored. Both must pass.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Drawn from real operational errors: escalating without evidence, speculating on cause to the customer, treating permissions as an audit trail, skipping health checks.
