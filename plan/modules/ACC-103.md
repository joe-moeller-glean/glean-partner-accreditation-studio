## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Exam + design review (tracker: `Exam + design review`)
- **Blueprint:** Exam: 28 items, 7 per domain (architecture/search, security/identity, network/environment, connectors/data), judgment-weighted. Design review: human-led, rubric-scored on completeness, prerequisite accuracy, risk handling, and defensibility. Both must pass.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Drawn from real architect errors: permissions at ingest, stale IP assumptions, skipping environment gates, connector scope shortcuts.
