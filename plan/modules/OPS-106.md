## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Nia with the customer's risk and compliance lead
- **Through-line situation:** An annual review asks whether AI usage is governed and evidenced
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** The compliance lead asks for evidence of who accessed what through AI last quarter. 'It's all permissioned' is not evidence.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Governance evidence pack assembles from real platform surfaces

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Support a governance, risk, or compliance review with real evidence
- `OBJ-02` — Explain the available audit and reporting surfaces
- `OBJ-03` — Identify which compliance questions require Glean rather than the partner
- `OBJ-04` — Prepare a recurring governance review pack

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Two sorts: surface-to-question mapping, and partner-answerable versus Glean-answerable.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Evidence pack assembly - the signature moment.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Unavailable-evidence response; branches show the cost of overstating capability.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Audit surfaces.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Knowledge check, 6 items (tracker: `Knowledge check`)
- **Blueprint:** OBJ-01 x2, OBJ-02 x2, OBJ-03 x1, OBJ-04 x1.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Claiming an audit capability that does not exist, answering a certification question outside partner scope, treating permissions as an audit trail.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Governance review pack template with the surface-to-question map and the escalation boundary.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Actual audit, reporting, and retention surfaces - tier-1 docs; do not imply reporting that does not exist
- Which compliance and certification questions only Glean can answer (cross-reference TEC-102 and the trust portal)
- Retention windows

Freshness window: 90 days (security and compliance posture).. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.

## 9. Risks, blockers, and SME questions

- High-consequence: overstating audit capability in a compliance review damages customer trust irreparably.
- `Proposed expansion`, no asset - net-new.
- Prerequisite for ACC-106; also depends on TEC-102.
- No owner - `needs_sme`; needs a security/compliance-aware reviewer.
