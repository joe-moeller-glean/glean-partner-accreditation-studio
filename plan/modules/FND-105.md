## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Dana, solution advisor, plus Alex the builder as a foil
- **Through-line situation:** Northwind has approved a pilot; the team must choose primitives for six concrete workflows without over-building
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Alex wants to build a custom connector. Dana thinks an action would do. One of them is about to waste three weeks.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Decision-tree traversal replays the learner's own path with the cost of the alternative shown

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Apply a documented decision rule to choose between search, assistant, prompt, agent, action, connector, and integration
- `OBJ-02` — Justify a primitive choice in terms of data freshness, write access, and governance
- `OBJ-03` — Identify the disqualifying condition that rules out each primitive
- `OBJ-04` — Recognize when two primitives are legitimately interchangeable and what should break the tie

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-FADE`** — Three-stage worked example: full trace, partial with blanks, learner-completed. Stage and answers persisted.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — The Alex-vs-Dana connector decision; each branch shows real cost (time, maintenance, governance) of the wrong pick.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Six workflows onto seven primitives, including one legitimately ambiguous case where the feedback names the tie-breaker.
- **`INT-COMPARE`** — Indexed-vs-live and read-vs-write comparisons.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Scenario exercise, rubric-scored (tracker: `Scenario exercise`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits primitive choices plus justifications for three workflows. Rubric: completeness, factual accuracy, fit to constraints, risk handling, communicability.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** For the embedded self-checks: defaulting to custom connectors, ignoring write requirements, ignoring data-freshness needs.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Primitive selection worksheet - the decision rule, the disqualifying condition per primitive, and the tie-breaker questions.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Current capability boundaries of each primitive - especially what actions can write and what connectors can index
- Data-freshness behavior for indexed vs live-query patterns
- Governance implications of each primitive
- Existing SCORM folder 'Choosing the right construct/primitives' - inventory before authoring

Freshness window: 90 days (product behavior).. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
