## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Capstone lab, rubric-scored (tracker: `Capstone lab`)
- **Blueprint:** Four deliverables: working agent (2+ tools, test cases), integration, evaluation set with results, governance and handoff package. Rubric-scored on functionality, correctness, testing rigor, governance, and documentation. Pass = 80% with no criterion insufficient.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Not applicable - lab assessment. The readiness self-check carries corrective feedback.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Capstone requirements checklist plus the full rubric, published in advance.
