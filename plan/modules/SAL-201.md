## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Marcus, now pitching to Northwind's executive committee
- **Through-line situation:** Marcus has 10 minutes with a CFO who has already rejected two AI proposals
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** The CFO opens with: 'The last two AI programs cost us money and changed nothing. Why is this different?'
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** The narrative spine assembles from the learner's own chosen beats into a 10-minute pitch outline

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Construct a C-suite narrative that connects an enterprise problem to a measurable outcome
- `OBJ-02` — Frame governance and risk as enablers rather than caveats
- `OBJ-03` — Defend the business case against a cost-and-credibility challenge
- `OBJ-04` — Deliver a 10-minute value pitch with a defensible close

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-FADE`** — Three-stage narrative construction: problem frame, outcome frame, close.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — CFO credibility challenge; branches show what happens when the seller defends with unanchored numbers versus a defensible range.
- **`INT-SEQ`** — Order the pitch beats; the signature reveal renders the learner's own outline.
- **`INT-COMPARE`** — Governance framed as enabler vs caveat.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Pitch exercise, rubric-scored (tracker: `Pitch exercise`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits a 10-minute pitch outline plus a recorded or written delivery. Rubric: narrative coherence, outcome specificity, factual accuracy, objection handling, executive appropriateness.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: leading with technology, using unanchored ROI figures, treating governance as a disclaimer, closing without a next step.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Executive pitch outline template with the four-part spine, the governance framing, and three CFO-objection responses.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Any ROI, savings, or adoption figure requires a tier-1/tier-2 anchor (PR-01) - this module is the highest overclaim risk in the Seller path
- Governance and security capabilities referenced in the pitch must match current product behavior
- Competitive framing must be verifiable and must not misstate a competitor

Freshness window: 90 days for product and metric claims; 180 days for program framing.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
