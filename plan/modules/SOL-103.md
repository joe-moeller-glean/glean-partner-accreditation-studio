## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Dana, building the business case with Northwind's finance analyst
- **Through-line situation:** The CFO wants a number, and Dana's inputs are all estimates
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Dana's ROI model says 340%. The CFO asks where every input came from. Three of the seven have no source.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** The value model recalculates live as the learner replaces each unsourced assumption with a defensible range

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Build a value model from inputs the customer can verify themselves
- `OBJ-02` — Distinguish a defensible estimate from an invented figure
- `OBJ-03` — Convert adoption and time-savings measures into a business-case narrative
- `OBJ-04` — Define how realized outcomes will be measured after go-live

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-FADE`** — Three-stage value model: worked, partial, then the learner replaces unsourced inputs with defensible ranges. Live recalculation is the signature moment.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — The single-number request; branches show the credibility cost of false precision.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Value chain stages.
- **`INT-COMPARE`** — Defensible estimate versus invention.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Model submission, rubric-scored (tracker: `Model submission`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits a value model with every input sourced or explicitly flagged as a customer-supplied assumption, plus a post-go-live measurement plan.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: using vendor-supplied averages as customer facts, presenting a point estimate without a range, omitting the measurement plan.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Value model template with an input-provenance column and a measurement plan.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Realized Outcomes playbook content - what is actually measured and how
- PR-01 applies with full force: no benchmark, percentage, or savings figure without a tier-1/tier-2 anchor

Freshness window: 90 days for any metric; 365 days for methodology.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
