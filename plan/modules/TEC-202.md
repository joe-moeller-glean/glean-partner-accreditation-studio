## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Sam and Nia the admin, post-launch
- **Through-line situation:** A customer claims results are wrong and there is no evidence either way
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** 'Search is broken.' No error, no ticket detail, no reproduction. The logs will decide who is right.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Evidence package assembles from scattered log events into one coherent timeline

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Locate and interpret the platform logs and activity events relevant to a complaint
- `OBJ-02` — Assemble an evidence package that supports a diagnosis
- `OBJ-03` — Design operational telemetry that catches problems before users report them
- `OBJ-04` — Distinguish a platform defect from a configuration or permission outcome

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-TRACE`** — Read a real trace table and isolate the cause; keyboard navigable, selection persisted.
- **`INT-SIM`** — Guided walkthrough of the activity event trail.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Evidence package assembly - the signature moment.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Symptoms classified as defect, configuration, or expected permission behavior.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Lab (tracker: `Lab`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner completes a diagnostic lab: given a scenario and log excerpts, produce a diagnosis plus a complete evidence package. Rubric-scored; SCORM records completion and rubric score.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: reporting a permission outcome as a defect, submitting evidence without timestamps or user context, escalating before checking configuration.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Evidence package template with the five required fields and the defect-vs-config decision rule.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Which logs and activity events exist and what each contains - tier-1 docs plus real captures
- Retention windows and access requirements for log surfaces
- What constitutes a complete escalation package for Glean support

Freshness window: 90 days (product surfaces and captures).. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.

## 9. Risks, blockers, and SME questions

- Captures must be aggressively redacted - log data is the highest PII risk in the portfolio.
- Partial asset (Logging & Activity Event logs); research must resolve the exact source and owner.
- Overlaps OPS-104 and SRV-204; TEC-202 owns telemetry design, OPS-104 owns triage process, SRV-204 owns agent-specific debugging.
- No owner - `needs_sme`.
