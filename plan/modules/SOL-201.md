## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Dana with Sam the architect
- **Through-line situation:** Discovery is done; Northwind wants to see the solution before they will fund it
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Northwind's committee will fund a blueprint they understand. Dana's current draft is a list of features.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** The blueprint assembles in four layers - users, workflows, systems, governance - into one page

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Turn discovery output into a four-layer solution blueprint
- `OBJ-02` — Tell the architecture story to a mixed business and technical audience
- `OBJ-03` — Show how governance and rollout are part of the design, not an appendix
- `OBJ-04` — Defend a blueprint against a scope and a risk challenge

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-FADE`** — Three-stage blueprint construction across four layers; the signature reveal renders the learner's own one-pager.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Scope and risk challenges with consequences for funding.
- **`INT-COMPARE`** — Business-audience versus technical-audience framing of the same blueprint.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Blueprint layers.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Blueprint review, rubric-scored (tracker: `Blueprint review`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits a one-page blueprint covering all four layers plus a rollout outline and one named risk with mitigation.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: omitting governance, describing systems without users, presenting a feature list as a blueprint.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- One-page blueprint template with the four layers and two audience framings.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Architecture elements referenced must match current platform reality (cross-reference TEC-101)
- Governance capabilities must be current (90-day window)
- Rollout patterns should align with the Platform Deployment & Growth playbook

Freshness window: 90 days for architecture and governance; 365 days for method.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.
