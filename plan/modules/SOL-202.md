## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Dana, now a practice lead packaging repeatable offerings
- **Through-line situation:** Dana has won three deals in different industries and wants a reusable pattern
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Three wins, three from-scratch discovery cycles. Dana is rebuilding the same solution every time with a different logo on it.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Pattern card flips: a generic pattern becomes a scoped, industry-specific play

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Identify the reusable structure inside an industry solution
- `OBJ-02` — Package a pattern with its assumptions, prerequisites, and disqualifiers
- `OBJ-03` — Adapt a pattern to a new industry without losing rigor
- `OBJ-04` — Decide when a pattern does not apply

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Separate reusable core components from industry-specific edges.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Two-stage pattern scoping; the signature flip renders the scoped play.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Pattern-does-not-fit decision with consequences for delivery risk.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Pattern card fields.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Knowledge check, 6 items (tracker: `Knowledge check`)
- **Blueprint:** OBJ-01 x2, OBJ-02 x2, OBJ-03 x1, OBJ-04 x1 (disqualifier judgment).
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Treating an industry example as a pattern, omitting prerequisites, reusing a pattern past its disqualifier.
