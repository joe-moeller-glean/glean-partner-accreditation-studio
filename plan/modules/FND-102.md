## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Priya, now committed to building the practice
- **Through-line situation:** Priya must enroll six people across sales, technical, and delivery roles and cannot tell which track each person needs
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Priya has six names and five learning paths. Pick wrong and her architects sit through seller content for a week.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Role-to-path map animates each of the six named teammates onto their correct track

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Map a partner role to the correct accreditation path and badge
- `OBJ-02` — Sequence the modules required for a chosen badge, respecting prerequisites
- `OBJ-03` — Navigate the partner LMS to enroll, resume, and evidence completion
- `OBJ-04` — Explain how badges are earned, maintained, and re-verified

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Six role cards (AE, SE, architect, consultant, admin, practice lead) routed to paths; feedback explains the routing rule, not just correctness.
- **`INT-SEQ`** — Order four modules into a valid prerequisite sequence; invalid orders explain which prerequisite was violated.
- **`INT-SIM`** — Six-step LMS walkthrough over real captures; step state persisted so a mid-walkthrough resume lands correctly.
- **`INT-CHECKLIST`** — Practice enablement plan the learner fills and downloads locally.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.
