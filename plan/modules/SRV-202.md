## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Ravi handing the platform to the customer
- **Through-line situation:** The project team is leaving and nobody owns the platform
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Ravi's team leaves in three weeks. Right now, if a connector breaks, the answer is 'ask Ravi'.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** CoE structure assembles: roles, cadence, and decision rights lock into place

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Design a Center of Excellence with defined roles and decision rights
- `OBJ-02` — Establish the governance operating cadence
- `OBJ-03` — Assign ownership for each platform responsibility
- `OBJ-04` — Produce a governance plan that survives the project team leaving
