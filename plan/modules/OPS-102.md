## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Nia with the partner's support engineer
- **Through-line situation:** An access failure at 9am with no obvious cause
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Forty users cannot see their own documents. Nothing changed on the Glean side. Something changed somewhere.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Runbook resolves: the failure isolates to one layer and the rollback path becomes clear

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Diagnose an access or authentication failure systematically
- `OBJ-02` — Execute the validation and rollback procedures
- `OBJ-03` — Determine the handoff point to Glean support
- `OBJ-04` — Maintain cloud operational runbooks for recurring failure modes

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-TRACE`** — Run the diagnostic sequence and isolate the failing layer - the signature moment.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Symptoms mapped to layers.
- **`INT-SIM`** — Validation and rollback execution.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Handoff-timing decision.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Quiz, 8 items (tracker: `Quiz`)
- **Blueprint:** OBJ-01 x3 (diagnostic reasoning), OBJ-02 x2, OBJ-03 x2, OBJ-04 x1.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Assuming a Glean-side cause, rolling back before diagnosing, escalating without evidence, treating an expired credential as a platform fault.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Cloud operational runbook with the diagnostic decision tree and rollback procedures.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify
