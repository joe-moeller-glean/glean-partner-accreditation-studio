## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Dana, solution advisor
- **Through-line situation:** Northwind's six departments each want something different from the same platform
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Six departments, six wish lists, one budget. Dana has to find the two that make the others possible.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Persona-to-outcome map resolves: six departments collapse onto three shared workflow patterns

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Map a department's pains, stakeholders, and outcomes without generic language
- `OBJ-02` — Identify the shared workflow pattern beneath superficially different departmental requests
- `OBJ-03` — Name the stakeholder who must sponsor a given outcome
- `OBJ-04` — Produce a department outcome worksheet a customer stakeholder would sign

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-FADE`** — Three-stage department mapping: IT worked, support partial, finance independent.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Six departmental requests sorted onto three shared workflow patterns - the signature payoff.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Sponsor identification with consequences for funding and adoption.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Department grid cells.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Worksheet, rubric-scored (tracker: `Worksheet`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits outcome maps for two departments: named pains, named stakeholder roles, measurable outcomes, and the shared pattern linking them.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: generic pain statements, mapping to features, naming a stakeholder who cannot fund.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Department outcome worksheet plus the shared-pattern shortlist.
