## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Dana as department specialist, with a hr and people stakeholder
- **Through-line situation:** A hr and people leader wants to know what changes in their team's daily work
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** An HR leader says policy questions consume a third of her team's week and the answers are already written down.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Department play card flips: the shared pattern becomes a hr and people-specific workflow set

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Name the hr and people workflows Glean addresses and the stakeholder who owns each
- `OBJ-02` — Connect a hr and people pain to a measurable outcome
- `OBJ-03` — Select the right demo surface and proof point for a hr and people audience
- `OBJ-04` — Adapt the departmental pattern to a specific customer without overclaiming

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Two sorts: stakeholder-to-workflow mapping, and proof-point selection.
- **`INT-SIM`** — Guided demonstration of the hr and people workflow over real captures.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — The characteristic hr and people objection.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Pattern adaptation - the signature play-card flip.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Scenario quiz, 8 items (tracker: `Scenario quiz`)
- **Blueprint:** OBJ-01 x2, OBJ-02 x2, OBJ-03 x3 (proof and demo selection), OBJ-04 x1. Every stem is a hr and people situation.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Generic productivity claims, demoing the wrong surface for the audience, unanchored time-savings figures, applying another department's pattern unchanged.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- HR and people play card: workflows, stakeholder map, demo surfaces, proof points, and disqualifiers.

## 8. Claims to establish and verify

- Named hr and people workflows and stakeholder roles from the Departmental Outcomes master deck
- Any time-savings, efficiency, or adoption figure requires a tier-1/tier-2 anchor (PR-01)
- Demo surfaces shown must match current product; captures stamped and 90-day re-verified
- Agents referenced as demonstrable must actually exist and be reachable

Freshness window: 365 days for workflow patterns; 90 days for product capability and captures.. Every factual sentence carries a `claim_id` resolving to `sources.json`.

## 9. Risks, blockers, and SME questions

- Highest sensitivity for permission and confidentiality framing - HR content is the classic example of why permission inheritance matters.
