## 5. Interactions

- **`quiz engine`** — 24-item exam, one item per view, no timer, reshuffled retries, explanatory feedback on final attempt, keyboard-complete.
- **`INT-CHECK`** — Readiness self-check that seeds the review list.
- **`INT-CHECKLIST`** — Role-play brief the learner takes into a human-observed session.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Exam + role play (tracker: `Exam + role play`)
- **Blueprint:** Exam: 24 items blueprinted across SAL-101/102/201/202/203/204 (6 per objective area), judgment-weighted for 200 level. Role play: human-observed, rubric-scored on narrative, discovery, objection handling, and time discipline. Both must pass.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Drawn from real partner errors across the Seller path: unanchored ROI, disparaging competitors, exceeding approval authority, demoing without a purpose statement.
