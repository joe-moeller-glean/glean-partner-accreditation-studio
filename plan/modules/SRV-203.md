## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Ravi's firm turning delivery into recurring revenue
- **Through-line situation:** The customer wants ongoing support and Ravi's firm has no service definition
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** The customer asks what an ongoing support retainer includes. Ravi's honest answer is 'whatever you need', which is unpriceable.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Service tiers resolve: scope, response, and price boundaries become explicit

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Define managed service tiers with explicit scope boundaries
- `OBJ-02` — Specify what run-state ownership includes and excludes
- `OBJ-03` — Design an optimization offering with measurable deliverables
- `OBJ-04` — Price and staff a support model realistically

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Service design artifact, rubric-scored (tracker: `Service design artifact`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner submits a service design: tiers with explicit scope, response expectations, deliverables, exclusions, and a staffing model.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: tiers without exclusions, response commitments without staffing, optimization without measurable deliverables.
