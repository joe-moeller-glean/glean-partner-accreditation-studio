## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Ravi designing the customer's enablement program
- **Through-line situation:** The customer expects Ravi's team to train 4,000 users
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Four thousand users, one enablement team of three. Training everyone individually is not a plan.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Champion network multiplies: three trainers become forty enabled advocates

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Design a scalable enablement program for a large user population
- `OBJ-02` — Build a champion model with defined responsibilities
- `OBJ-03` — Sequence enablement against the adoption curve
- `OBJ-04` — Measure enablement effectiveness rather than attendance
