## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Dana (solution advisor) and Sam (architect) together
- **Through-line situation:** Northwind asks for 'an AI assistant', but three of their five requests are actually agent work
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Five Northwind requests on one slide. Two are search. Two are agents. One cannot be built the way they asked. Which is which?
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** The construct ladder resolves: five requests slide into their correct construct with the reasoning trace visible

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Define search, assistant, and agent in terms of what each decides and executes
- `OBJ-02` — Place a customer request on the correct construct given its trigger, autonomy, and side effects
- `OBJ-03` — Explain where governance and human approval enter an agentic workflow
- `OBJ-04` — Describe how the constructs compose in one customer outcome

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Two full decision branches on real Northwind requests; each branch explains the downstream consequence of choosing the wrong construct.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Final five-request sort - the signature payoff. Keyboard-first, live-region announced, per-item reasoning feedback.
- **`INT-COMPARE`** — Assistant vs agent columns, both readable without the slider.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Agent anatomy and approval-gate diagrams.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Quiz, 9 items (tracker: `Quiz`)
- **Blueprint:** OBJ-01 x2 recall, OBJ-02 x4 application (request -> construct), OBJ-03 x2 judgment (approval placement), OBJ-04 x1.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Treating any conversational surface as an agent, assuming agents need no approval design, assuming search cannot answer multi-step questions.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- 'Which construct?' decision card - trigger, autonomy, side effects, approval requirement, and the disqualifying condition for each construct.
