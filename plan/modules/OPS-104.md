## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Nia's support engineer mid-incident
- **Through-line situation:** A live incident with three plausible causes and a customer waiting
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Three plausible causes, one customer on the line, and the wrong first guess costs an hour.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Triage tree resolves: evidence eliminates two causes and confirms the third

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Triage an incident using evidence rather than intuition
- `OBJ-02` — Isolate a cause by elimination
- `OBJ-03` — Route the incident to the correct owner with a complete package
- `OBJ-04` — Communicate status to the customer during an active incident

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-TRACE`** — Evidence gathering from real diagnostic output.
- **`INT-SORT`** — Eliminate causes by evidence - the signature resolution.
- **`INT-BRANCH`** — Routing decision.
- **`INT-FADE`** — Customer status update, worked then independent.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** Scenario exercise, rubric-scored (tracker: `Scenario exercise`)
- **Blueprint:** Learner works a live-incident scenario: evidence gathered, causes eliminated with justification, correct routing, and a customer-appropriate status update.
- **Mastery:** 80%, retries reshuffled, explanatory feedback on final attempt
- **Distractor sources:** Embedded checks target: guessing before gathering evidence, routing without a package, status updates that speculate on cause.

## 7. Job aid / carry-away

- Incident triage runbook with the elimination checklist and a status-update template.
