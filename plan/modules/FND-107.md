## 1. Narrative frame

- **Recurring persona:** Priya's six enrolled teammates, about to sit their first assessment
- **Through-line situation:** The team does not know what the exams look like, what a lab requires, or what happens on a fail
- **Cold open (A1) hook:** Two of Priya's six failed their first attempt - not on knowledge, but on format surprise.
- **Signature moment (one T3 reveal):** Badge wall reveals all eight accreditations with the learner's own position marked

## 2. Learning outcomes (observable)

- `OBJ-01` — Describe what each assessment format requires and how it is scored
- `OBJ-02` — State the mastery threshold, retry rules, and what feedback appears when
- `OBJ-03` — Prepare evidence for a lab or artifact-based accreditation
- `OBJ-04` — Explain how accreditation is maintained after it is earned

## 5. Interactions

- **`INT-SORT`** — Match eight named modules to their assessment format; feedback explains what each format demands of the learner.
- **`INT-COMPARE`** — Four assessment formats side by side with what is scored and how.
- **`INT-HOTSPOT`** — Rubric anatomy - what each criterion means in practice.
- **`INT-CHECKLIST`** — Evidence checklist the learner downloads before attempting a lab.
- Keyboard-equivalent path required in every component; state persisted to `cmi.suspend_data`; restores exactly on resume.

## 6. Assessment plan

- **Format:** None (tracker: `None`) - orientation only (tracker: `None`)
- **Blueprint:** No scored assessment. The module still writes completion status and tracks the self-check and checklist state. Do not invent an exam here.
- **Mastery:** Not applicable - completion-tracked only
- **Distractor sources:** Not applicable; the sort interaction carries the corrective feedback instead.

- Actual assessment formats in use per module (derivable from the tracker's Assessment column - 28 distinct values)
