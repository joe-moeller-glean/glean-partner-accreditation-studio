# CON-101 assessment rubric

Mastery score: 80%. Items are single-select with explanatory feedback on every option.

## Objective coverage

| Objective | Items |
|---|---|
| `OBJ-01` Explain what a connector does and the data families it brings into Glean. | Q-01, Q-02 |
| `OBJ-02` Distinguish native, web history, push API, and partner connectors, including who builds and supports each. | Q-03, Q-04 |
| `OBJ-03` Choose indexed, live, or hybrid access from the data and the question. | Q-05, Q-06, Q-07 |
| `OBJ-04` Explain where tools and MCP fit, and what they do not do. | Q-08, Q-09, Q-10 |
| `OBJ-05` Decide whether a custom connector is justified, using a five-question fit sequence. | Q-11, Q-12 |

## Item-writing rules applied

- Every item tests a claim-backed fact or a decision derived from one, never wording recall.
- Distractors are plausible partner mistakes drawn from the source material, not absurd options.
- Feedback explains why an option is wrong, not merely that it is wrong.
- Answer keys live in `key.json` and are never present in `items.json`.

## Calibration

Review live pass rates after 30 completions. Flag any item below 30% or above 95% for rewrite.
