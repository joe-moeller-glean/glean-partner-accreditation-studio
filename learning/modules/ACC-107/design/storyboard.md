# ACC-107 Custom Connector Competency Capstone - storyboard

## 1. SCR-01 - A1 - Cold open
**Defend it**

You have scoped, designed, built, broken, and operated a connector. Now two reviewers will ask why, for every choice, and probe the places connectors usually fail. The capstone is not a quiz about the connector. It is proof that you can build one someone else will trust.

## 2. SCR-02 - A5 - Two credentials
**Know what this badge is, and is not**

The Custom Connector Competency certifies partners to scope, build, deploy, and maintain custom data source integrations with Glean. Competencies require thorough experience and an audit process. This course's accreditation prepares you and your evidence for that audit; it does not award the competency.

Claims: C-260, C-267, C-266
