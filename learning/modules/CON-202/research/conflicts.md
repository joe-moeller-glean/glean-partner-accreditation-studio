# CON-202 - conflicts, exclusions, and open questions

## Authority ruling

Current Glean product and developer documentation (tier 1) is authoritative for product behavior. Partner enablement and program documents (tier 2) supply program rules and practice guidance that tier 1 does not contradict. Tier 3 and 4 material is excluded.

## Open questions carried by this module

- Partner access to Glean-hosted deployment for partner-built connectors is unconfirmed; the module tells learners to confirm with their Partner Manager.
- Bulk-upload endpoint rate limit is not in the reviewed sources; sizing uses documented /indexdocument and /indexdocuments limits only.
