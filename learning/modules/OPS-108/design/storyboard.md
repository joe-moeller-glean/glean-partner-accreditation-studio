## 3. SCR-03 - A3 - The community
**Gleaniverse is Glean's customer community**

Gleaniverse is Glean's primary customer community: a secure space to ask product questions, share feedback, and discover best practices. Register with a work email so you receive the correct role and permissions. The home page offers three starting points: Start a Discussion, Join a Group, and Explore Academy.

## 5. SCR-05 - A3 - The routing rule
**Community for shared answers. Tickets for your instance.**

For bugs and errors in a Glean instance, file a support ticket. For questions, best practices, or feature requests, use the community. Community answers let Support help multiple customers at once.
