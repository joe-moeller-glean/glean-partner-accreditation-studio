## Claims by source

### S-101 - Glean Support Center (Glean Docs)

- `C-224` Submit feature requests and product feedback on the Feedback and Ideas board in Gleaniverse.

### S-103 - Tickets (Glean Docs)

- `C-212` Priority levels are Low, Normal, High, and Urgent. Low covers non-critical issues such as general product inquiries and feature requests. Over-escalating priority can slow resolution for genuinely critical issues.

### S-105 - Common Internal FAQs on Fin Support Migration

- `C-225` Glean Support runs a monthly live troubleshooting session on the second Thursday of each month, working through questions in small groups.

### S-106 - Gleaniverse Community (home page)

- `C-302` The Gleaniverse home page offers three starting points: Start a Discussion, Join a Group, and Explore Academy.
- `C-301` Gleaniverse navigation includes Forum, Resources, Support Center, Events, Groups, Ask Query, and Academy.
- `C-316` Glean Academy in Gleaniverse offers courses to master new topics.

### S-107 - Gleaniverse Community Guidelines

- `C-306` Look before you post: search first, because the question may already be answered.
- `C-307` Keep sensitive information out: do not share confidential or personal data.
- `C-308` Keep it relevant: focus on Glean and the broader AI industry. No spam, self-promotion, bots, or unsolicited outreach.
- `C-309` Be kind and make it a safe space. Harassment, hate speech, or unwelcoming behavior is not tolerated.
- `C-310` Make it better and build together: share feedback and learnings, and help others when they need it.
- `C-311` Glean may remove content or restrict access for guideline violations. Report anything that does not feel right to the Glean team.

### S-108 - Events - Gleaniverse

- `C-225` Glean Support runs a monthly live troubleshooting session on the second Thursday of each month, working through questions in small groups.
- `C-312` The Glean Product Team hosts monthly product updates on the fourth Thursday of each month.
- `C-313` Replays of previous Glean webinars and events are available on demand.

### S-109 - Community Toolkit (Gleaniverse Toolkit)

- `C-303` Gleaniverse is Glean's primary customer community: a secure space to ask product questions, share feedback, and discover best practices.
- `C-304` Register for Gleaniverse with a work email so you receive the correct role and permissions.
- `C-315` The Admin Hub is a restricted space for customer administrators.
- `C-305` For bugs and errors in a Glean instance, file a support ticket. For questions, best practices, or feature requests, use the community.
- `C-314` Community answers let Support help multiple customers at once and can reduce inbound volume.
