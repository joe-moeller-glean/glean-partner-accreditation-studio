## Claims by source

### S-01 - Glean Partner Competency Program Guide

- `C-01` The Glean Agent Building Competency certifies partner teams to design, build, govern, and operationalize production-grade Glean Agents that automate workflows and deliver measurable business outcomes.
- `C-02` The Agent Building Competency focuses on persona-aligned agent design, prompt engineering, tool use, governance, and operational best practices across the agent lifecycle.
- `C-03` One competency requirement is completing 5 technical accreditations within the Glean Partner Portal, validated by a Glean Partner Manager.
- `C-04` One competency requirement is completing a Glean-delivered agent building workshop session with your Glean technical team, validated by a Glean Partner Solutions Engineer.
- `C-05` The competency requires building 5 functioning, production-ready agents.
- `C-06` Each of the 5 competency agents must implement at least 3 discrete steps beyond simple retrieval, including at least one thinking or transformation step.
- `C-07` Each of the 5 competency agents must configure retrieval guardrails so the agent uses a well-defined set of content sources and has clear instructions for what it can and cannot answer.
- `C-08` Each of the 5 competency agents must contain explicit error-handling with a defined fallback behavior for low-confidence or out-of-scope questions.
- `C-09` At least 1 of the 5 competency agents must invoke actions or tools that write to other systems.
- `C-10` At least 1 of the 5 competency agents must call more than one tool or system in a single run.
- `C-11` At least 1 of the 5 competency agents must use conditional branching.
- `C-12` At least 1 of the 5 competency agents must use loops to process a collection.
- `C-13` At least 1 of the 5 competency agents must use a schedule-based trigger.
- `C-14` At least 1 of the 5 competency agents must use an event or content-based trigger.
- `C-15` For each competency agent, partners must gather business and technical requirements with the end user, evidenced by a document outlining those requirements.
- `C-16` Competency validation includes a call with a Glean Partner Solutions Engineer to review the finalized agents; partners should expect to walk through and run each agent on the call, and ideally provide a recorded walkthrough beforehand.
- `C-17` For each competency agent, partners must complete an agent architecture review with a Glean technical resource, outlining best practices followed and agent structure.
- `C-18` For each competency agent, partners must provide a written outline of the agent's business impact and why a Glean Agent was the best solution, reviewed by a Glean Partner Solutions Engineer.
- `C-22` Competency benefits include market visibility as a trusted Glean Partner, a Glean Partner Competency Badge, access to Glean services opportunities, being surfaced to Glean sellers as a preferred services partner, and a searchable directory listing.
- `C-23` Agent Building Competency benefits include priority consideration for agent-focused services opportunities and co-marketing opportunities that highlight agent outcomes and ROI.
- `C-24` The Competency Program is open to all registered Glean Partners, including resell, services, and ISV or product partners, and the organization must already be a registered Glean Partner.
- `C-25` Glean competencies require thorough experience and successful completion of an audit process.
- `C-26` The Agent Building Competency Submission Workbook is available in the Glean Partner Portal Asset Library.
