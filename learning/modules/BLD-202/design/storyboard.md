# BLD-202 Custom Connector Implementation Lab - storyboard

## 1. SCR-01 - A1 - Cold open
**Done is not when the data appears**

Requirements to production handoff in one lab. The documents will appear early. That is not the finish line. The lab is done when you can prove who sees what, what happens when things change or break, and when someone else can maintain it without you.

## 3. SCR-03 - A3 - The floor
**The competency build minimum**

The Custom Connector Competency expects a build that indexes users, permissions, and documents or data; has some level of automated index updating; and handles errors with retry and backoff. That is the floor. This lab goes further, because production connectors fail in the places the minimum does not name.
