## 3. SCR-03 - A3 - The support path
**Everything starts in Gleaniverse**

You must sign in to Gleaniverse to reach the Glean Support Center. Logins from the legacy support portal were not migrated. After you sign in, there are two entry points: Ask Query in the top navigation, and the support widget in the bottom-right corner. Before you open a ticket, search Glean Docs, ask Query, and check the Glean status page for known issues.

Claims: C-201, C-202, C-203, C-204

## 4. SCR-04 - A3 - Two ways to get help
**Conversations and tickets do different jobs**

Conversations are available to all users and are the best starting point for most requests. Tickets formally track issues that need follow-up or escalation, such as bug reports and outages. If a conversation needs escalation, support can convert it into a ticket.

## 10. SCR-10 - A3 - Why the portal matters
**Only portal incidents start the SLA clock**

Only incidents submitted through the Glean support portal are eligible for SLA response times. Response times cannot be tracked accurately outside the portal. The customer's Order Form sets the support tier.
