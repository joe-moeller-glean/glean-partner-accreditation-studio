## Claims by source

### S-101 - Glean Support Center (Glean Docs)

- `C-201` You must sign in to Gleaniverse to access the Glean Support Center.
- `C-202` Logins from the legacy support portal were not migrated, so users need a Gleaniverse account.
- `C-203` Before opening a ticket, search Glean Docs, ask Query, and check the Glean status page for known issues.
- `C-204` The Support Center has two entry points: Ask Query in the top navigation and the support widget in the bottom-right corner.
- `C-205` Conversations are available to all users and are the best starting point for most requests.
- `C-207` Tickets formally track issues that need follow-up or escalation. They are best for bug reports, outages, and requests that need a documented resolution.
- `C-208` If a conversation needs escalation, support can convert it into a ticket.
- `C-222` Email to help@glean.com is triaged by the AI assistant first and handed to a human agent for Designated Support Contacts.
- `C-224` Submit feature requests and product feedback on the Feedback and Ideas board in Gleaniverse.

### S-102 - Conversations (Glean Docs)

- `C-202` Logins from the legacy support portal were not migrated, so users need a Gleaniverse account.
- `C-205` Conversations are available to all users and are the best starting point for most requests.
- `C-206` To start a conversation, open the support widget and select Send us a message. The assistant triages the request and guides you to a ticket if it cannot resolve the issue.

### S-103 - Tickets (Glean Docs)

- `C-207` Tickets formally track issues that need follow-up or escalation. They are best for bug reports, outages, and requests that need a documented resolution.
- `C-209` Ticket creation is available to Designated Support Contacts as defined in the Glean Support Agreement. Contact the Glean account team to be set up as one.
- `C-213` To create a ticket, a Designated Support Contact opens the support widget, selects Create a ticket, describes the issue, selects a priority, and submits.
- `C-210` Users who are not Designated Support Contacts can start a conversation or ask their Glean administrator to submit a ticket.
- `C-211` A complete ticket includes an issue description with reproduction steps, exact error messages, date and time with timezone, user impact, the relevant document URL, and screenshots or recordings.
- `C-212` Priority levels are Low, Normal, High, and Urgent. Low covers non-critical issues such as general product inquiries and feature requests. Over-escalating priority can slow resolution for genuinely critical issues.
- `C-215` Designated Support Contacts view company-wide conversations and tickets through the Go to customer portal banner in the support widget. Do not go directly to ticket.glean.com.
- `C-214` The Tickets section in the support widget appears only after the first ticket is created.

### S-104 - Glean Customer Support SLA (v6Mar2026)

- `C-219` The customer must assess business impact and urgency when selecting a priority.
- `C-220` Urgent examples: the Service is unavailable to all users, or a security incident is detected. High examples: the Service is unavailable to a subset of users, or a new release materially harmed existing connected applications. Normal example: results are incomplete or truncated from known connected sources.
- `C-216` Only incidents submitted through the Glean support portal are eligible for SLA response times. Response times cannot be tracked accurately outside the portal.
- `C-217` Standard Support initial response times: Urgent 1 business day, High 2 business days, Normal and Low 1 business week. Premium: Urgent 1 hour, High 4 business hours, Normal 1 business day, Low 2 business days.
- `C-218` Standard Support provides 24x5 hours and 1 to 4 Designated Support Contacts. Premium provides 24x7 for Urgent incidents only and 1 to 10 Designated Support Contacts. The Order Form sets the tier.
- `C-221` Designated Support Contacts should know the deployment and configuration, have admin access to connected applications, prioritize accurately, and use self-service resources first.

### S-105 - Common Internal FAQs on Fin Support Migration

- `C-223` Non-entitled users can be cc'd on a conversation or ticket with a Designated Support Contact, for example to supply screenshots or reproduction steps.
- `C-225` Glean Support runs a monthly live troubleshooting session on the second Thursday of each month, working through questions in small groups.
