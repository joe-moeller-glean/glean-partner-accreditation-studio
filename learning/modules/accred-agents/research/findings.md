## Claims by source

### S-01 - Glean Partner Competency Program Guide

- `C-01` The Glean Agent Building Competency certifies partner teams to design, build, govern, and operationalize production-grade Glean Agents that automate workflows and deliver measurable business outcomes.
- `C-03` One competency requirement is completing 5 technical accreditations within the Glean Partner Portal, validated by a Glean Partner Manager.
- `C-04` One competency requirement is completing a Glean-delivered agent building workshop session with your Glean technical team, validated by a Glean Partner Solutions Engineer.
- `C-05` The competency requires building 5 functioning, production-ready agents.
- `C-15` For each competency agent, partners must gather business and technical requirements with the end user, evidenced by a document outlining those requirements.
- `C-19` Competency graduates can design and implement persona-aligned agents, define intents, configure retrieval and guardrails, and construct multi-step agents with clear handoffs and fallbacks.
- `C-20` Advanced agent capabilities include schedule-based triggers, event-driven runs on content changes, and conditional branching and looping to orchestrate robust workflows.
- `C-27` Competency graduates understand best practices for testing and validation of Glean agents.
- `C-30` Competency graduates can deliver enablement and advisory on agents and AI actions.

### S-05 - Partner Accreditation & Competency Roadmap - Module Tracker

- `C-88` FND-104 explains the Glean operating model for search, assistants, and agents, and where each construct fits for customer outcomes.
- `C-89` FND-105 provides a decision framework for when to use search, assistant, prompt, agent, action, connector, or integration pattern.
- `C-90` BLD-101 introduces what can be built with agents, actions, APIs, and integration points.
- `C-91` BLD-102 explains actions, MCP, architecture choices, and when to use each approach.
- `C-92` BLD-103 is a hands-on workshop building actions and MCP-backed flows.
- `C-93` BLD-104 is a deeper workshop on agent composition, memory, tool selection, and reusable patterns.
- `C-94` BLD-207 covers how to evaluate prompts and agents, measure quality, and prevent regressions.
- `C-95` BLD-208 covers safety patterns, approval design, escalation boundaries, and production governance for partner-built agents.
- `C-96` SRV-204 covers trace-driven debugging, issue isolation, and clean escalation packages.
- `C-97` SOL-203 designs industry-specific agents that map to common workflows, data sources, and governance requirements.
- `C-98` ACC-104 is the badge for builders who can implement actions, agents, and connectors, assessed by a capstone lab.
